# Note to the Paylane Head of Support

**Subject: Your support assistant: what it does, what it won't do, and what we need from you**

Hi,

The first version of your support assistant is ready for you to try. Here is a short summary.

## What it does
- **Checks who it is talking to first.** Before it talks about any account, the customer has to give the email on file and the last 4 digits of their card. It never shows a full card number or the email address.
- **Tries to fix the problem before refunding.** If a customer doesn't recognise a charge, it explains what the charge is (for example, their own family music plan). If a "charge" is really a temporary hold, such as a hotel deposit, it explains that the hold will drop off. If someone forgot to cancel a subscription, it shows them how to cancel. If an order never arrived, it suggests tracking it or contacting the shop first.
- **Refunds small charges on the spot** when the problem can't be fixed another way. The charge has to be $75 or less and less than 30 days old.
- **Hands everything else to your team** and tells the customer, "A specialist will follow up by email."

## What it won't do
- Refund charges over $75 or 30 days or older. It doesn't do partial refunds on bigger charges either. These go to your team.
- Refund the same charge twice.
- Unfreeze a card. It can explain why a card was frozen, then it passes the case to your team.
- Handle fraud claims, legal threats or account closures. These always go to a person.
- Promise a callback time, a manager or anything else the policy doesn't allow, however upset the customer is.

## What we need from you before launch
1. **Please confirm or correct these choices.** We had to decide them ourselves:
   - Per customer, refunds made by the assistant add up to **no more than $75 in any 30 days**. This stops someone splitting a big charge into small refund requests.
   - **Every** fraud claim goes to a person, not only claims over $500.
   - Charges that are still **pending** can be refunded.
   - A charge from **exactly 30 days ago** counts as too old.
   - After **3 wrong verification attempts**, the chat stops sharing information and passes the case to your team.
   - The assistant tries to **solve the problem first** and refunds only when it can't, or when the customer still wants their money back for a fair reason.
   - It never gives a timeline, only "a specialist will follow up by email."
2. **What can it promise about follow-up?** If you have a standard reply time, we can use it.
3. **Your own wording** for the common fixes: how to cancel a subscription, how long holds take to drop off, and what to do about missing orders.
4. **A trial run.** Have two or three of your agents try it for a day and tell us anything that feels off, in tone or in decisions.

It is tested, but it isn't perfect. In our last test run it handled all 21 of our practice conversations correctly. We will keep checking real chats after launch.
